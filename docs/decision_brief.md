# Decision brief — hypothesis and investigation plan

**Status:** Working draft; not a finalized findings report.

**Scope:** An intentionally synthetic Ontario automobile-insurance portfolio covering 2024–2026.

## Hypothesis

Modeled 2026 increases in claim frequency for Toronto and GTA Other Collision and Accident Benefits may cause loss-ratio deterioration despite growth in premiums.

## Approach

1. Compare earned premium, incurred losses and loss ratios year over year by region and coverage.
2. Disentangle shifts in frequency and severity, accounting for earned exposure and portfolio mix.
3. Prioritize cells by estimated financial materiality, not only the largest ratio.
4. Verify completeness, unique observation keys, positive denominators and consistent coverage-level exposure definitions.
5. Contrast DAX measures with independent Python summaries.

## Guardrails

These descriptive outputs do **not** support immediate actual pricing recommendations. A real decision would require further investigation of reserves and loss development, claim maturity, exposure comparability, portfolio mix, statistical credibility and applicable regulatory constraints.

## Limitations

All observations and modeled shocks are fictional. No insurer or policyholder data was accessed. Patterns were intentionally created in a teaching dataset and do not establish real-world causes or outcomes.
